THE BACKROOMS NETWORK

You are now connected to the invisible wifi-scape of
The Backrooms.

Leave an anonymous message. Your words will echo through this liminal network, joining the whispers of those who passed through before.

The Backrooms Network (2025) by Antonio Hofmeister Ribeiro & Liudmila Savelyeva transforms abandoned Wi-Fi signals into a space for anonymous, collective expression. Visitors are invited to connect to local offline networks, leaving digital traces that form a ghostly, participatory archive—making the invisible architecture of connectivity visible, tactile, and communal.
