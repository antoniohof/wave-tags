DIGITAL TRACES

Invisible Networks in Abandoned Spaces

Welcome to the wifi-scape of an abandoned space. Here, in the invisible dimension of electromagnetic spectrum, new forms of collective publishing emerge.

Like the physical corridors of this ex-office space, the wifi networks that once served workers now lie dormant. Our installation reactivates this invisible infrastructure, transforming it into a territory for anonymous digital expression.

Your messages become part of a decentralized network of voices—anonymous traces left by visitors who have encountered these glowing devices. Like graffiti on abandoned walls, these digital tags accumulate over time, creating a collective narrative of presence in absence.

The stalagmite-like objects stand in the dim light, pulsing softly with a quiet energy. They feel like relics from a lost, post-digital world — both mysterious artifacts and working devices. Their glow and semi-transparent surfaces recall the crystal-books in Stanisław Lem’s science-fiction novel Return from the Stars (1991), where knowledge is stored in prisms and read by special machines. In the same way, these forms seem to hold traces of communication, crystallizing messages and technology into bridges between the physical and the digital.

Each message contributes to a growing archive of anonymous expression. Your words join a chorus of digital voices that exist simultaneously in the local wifi-scape and in the online mirror of this installation. This creates a form of collective publishing that is both site-specific and globally accessible.

How It Works

When you submit a message, it becomes a wifi network name visible to anyone scanning for networks in this area. Your trace propagates through the electromagnetic spectrum, creating a temporary constellation of thoughts, feelings, and expressions floating in the abandoned space.

About the Artists

Antonio Hofmeister Ribeiro & Liudmila Savelyeva

This work builds upon the original "Wave Tags" project, reimagined for The Backrooms exhibition as an exploration of how digital infrastructure can activate abandoned spaces and create new forms of collective expression.
